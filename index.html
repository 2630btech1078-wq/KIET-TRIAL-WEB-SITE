About Us

KIET is a Deemed to be University known for academic excellence, industry-aligned education, and strong national rankings. The Institute holds NAAC A+ accreditation, NBA-accredited programs, a QS I-GAUGE Diamond rating, and DSIR–SIRO recognition. KIET is renowned for industry-linked curriculum, and outstanding placements. The campus hosts advanced Centres of Excellence, a government-recognized Technology Business Incubator, and advanced research facilities. Ranked in NIRF and IIRF, KIET promotes innovation, entrepreneurship, and holistic student development.
